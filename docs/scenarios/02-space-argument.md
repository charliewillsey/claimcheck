# Scenario 02: Space argument

**DRAFT LABELS.** Written by the build agent. Not confirmed by Malcolm or Charlie.

## Script

A: I watched this space documentary last night. It was so good.
B: Oh nice, was it the one with the astronauts?
A: Yeah. [c1] You know you can see the Great Wall of China from the moon, right?
B: I do not buy that at all. Space stuff is wild though. [c2] Sunlight takes about eight minutes to reach Earth.
A: That is crazy to think about.
B: I think Mars is the coolest planet, honestly.
A: Mars is fine, but I like the hot ones. [c3] Mercury is the hottest planet because it is the closest to the sun.
B: Hmm, are you sure about that one?
A: Pretty sure. I remember it from school.
B: My favorite part of those shows is the astronauts floating around.
A: Same. [c4] They float because there is no gravity up on the space station.
B: I would throw up immediately. I cannot even do roller coasters.
A: Ha, same. Oh, here is a weird one. [c5] A single day on Venus is longer than a whole year on Venus.
B: Now I know you are capping.
A: I swear. Look it up.
B: I guess that is what this app is for.

## Labels

### c1
- claim: You can see the Great Wall of China from the moon.
- verdict: disputed
- source: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/great-wall/
- opened: plain fetch
- evidence: Despite myths to the contrary, the wall isn't visible from the moon

### c2
- claim: Sunlight takes about eight minutes to reach Earth.
- verdict: supported
- source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/
- opened: plain fetch
- evidence: It takes about eight minutes for light from the Sun to reach our planet.

### c3
- claim: Mercury is the hottest planet in the solar system.
- verdict: disputed
- source: https://science.nasa.gov/venus/venus-facts/
- opened: plain fetch
- evidence: Venus is the second planet from the Sun, and our closest planetary neighbor. It's the hottest planet in our solar system

### c4
- claim: Astronauts float because there is no gravity on the space station.
- verdict: disputed
- source: https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/for-kids-and-students/what-is-microgravity-grades-5-8/
- opened: plain fetch
- evidence: At that altitude, Earth's gravity is about 90 percent of what it is on the planet's surface.

### c5
- claim: A single day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus.
- verdict: supported
- source: https://science.nasa.gov/venus/venus-facts/
- opened: plain fetch
- evidence: your "day" would be 243 Earth days long — longer even than a Venus year (one trip around the Sun), which takes only 225 Earth days.
