# ROADMAP (post-MVP — do not build yet)

- **Personal source library:** users dump articles/sources; share-sheet connectors for Instagram, TikTok, etc.
- **Predictive pre-fetch:** model learns a speaker's language trends, predicts the article they're about to reference, queues it; second index-matching/validation layer decides whether to surface it.
- **Phone/OS mode:** turn it on like a VPN or call-record during a phone call; long-term, Apple-level adoption.
- **Echo-chamber guardrails:** prevent the personal library from becoming a tool for building one-sided ideologies.
- **Scale the eval:** more scenarios, push agreement from 75% to 90%.
